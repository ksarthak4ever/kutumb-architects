About Kutumb Architects

Our Story

Founded in 2018, Kutumb Architects has grown from a small design studio to a full-service architectural firm with a reputation for innovative design and meticulous attention to detail. Our team of passionate architects and designers is committed to creating spaces that not only meet but exceed our clients' expectations.

We believe that great architecture emerges from understanding our clients' unique needs, the site's context, and the surrounding environment. Our collaborative approach ensures that every project reflects the client's vision while incorporating sustainable and functional design principles.

Banquet Cum Marriage Garden

Hospitality

We are currently developing a landmark banquet and hospitality destination designed to seamlessly blend celebration, leisure, and luxury. The project envisions a grand marriage garden complemented by three spacious banquet halls, thoughtfully planned to accommodate gatherings of varying scales—from intimate family events to large ceremonial functions. To enhance the guest experience, the facility also integrates 60+ elegantly designed rooms, offering modern comforts and convenience for extended stays during weddings and celebrations. At the heart of the development, a swimming pool and landscaped green courts introduce a resort-like atmosphere, ensuring relaxation and recreation alongside festivities. Careful attention has been given to zoning, circulation, and aesthetics, ensuring smooth functionality without compromising the sense of grandeur. This ongoing site is conceived as more than just a venue—it is envisioned as a destination for timeless memories, where architecture supports cultural traditions while elevating hospitality standards.
